Lineage

Descendants of Dewji Bapa

વંશવૃક્ષ

262 family members over twelve generations, roughly three centuries recorded by name. Dewji Bapa had two sons, Samji and Narshi. Narshi’s sons Trikam, Vela and Ranchhod head the large branches.

    P = pedhi (generation) Italic = daughter ⚭ spouse # = permanent number, quote it when sending a correction

    Estimate How far back does our line go? Hirji Bapa, in the eighth generation, was born in 1905. Allowing 25 to 30 years for each generation before him, Dewji Bapa would have been born around the early 1700s, some 300 years ago.

    Birth years of living family members are not shown on this website. The full record stays in the printed book.
