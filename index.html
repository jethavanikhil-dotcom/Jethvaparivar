Time

Legends and turning points

Legend

Born of Hanuman

Tradition names Makardhwaj, son of Hanuman-ji, as our first ancestor, and his grandson Jethi-dhwaj as the one who gave us our name.

1193

The storm from the north

By the clan’s tradition Morvi is lost, and the Jethwas turn west to the Barda hills to begin again.

c.1220

Rana of Ghumli

Rana Shiyaji makes Ghumli the capital. The temples and stepwells rise around this time.

1313

The night Ghumli fell

Barmaniyaji defeats Rana Bhanji Jethwa, and the golden capital is left in ruins.

1671

Porbandar won back

Rana Vikmatji takes the port back from Mughal control and builds a fort.

c.1700

Dewji Bapa

Counting back from Hirji Bapa, born 1905, the founder of our own line was born around the early 1700s.

1947

Into the Union

Maharaja Rana Natwarsinhji signs Porbandar into India.

2026
