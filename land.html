Gazetteer

The places in this archive

  • Morvic. 900. Remembered in the clan’s tradition as an early capital, lost in 1193.
  • The Barda hillsWhere the clan began again after Morvi, near today’s Porbandar.
  • GhumliJethwa capital from about 1220 until 1313. The Navlakha temple and the Vikia Vav.
  • Ranpur and ChhayaThe Ranas’ seats after Ghumli fell, through the 1500s and 1600s.
  • PorbandarCapital of the Jethwa Ranas from 1685 to 1948. Birthplace of Mahatma Gandhi.
  • Bet DwarkaThe Hanuman Dandi temple, honouring Hanuman-ji and Makardhwaj together.
  • KhambhaliaOur ancestral shrine of Chorwad Mata and Sikotra Mata. Read more
  • ChorwadThe best-known temple of Chorwad Mata, also called Zund Bhavani, on the coast of Junagadh district.
  • MorzarThe shrine of our Sura Pura Bapa, Kachra Bapa. How to visit
