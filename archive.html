A book of many hands

No single person made this archive. It has been carried, piece by piece, by the whole family.

  1. On paper
    For years our family tree was kept by hand, in Gujarati. A paper copy was found by Dinesh Vallabhdas Jethwa and passed on to Dharmesh Babulal Jethwa.
  2. A modern tree
    Dharmesh gave it a modern form, reaching out to relatives and adding names, branch by branch.
  3. In Khambhalia
    Vaidik Vimal Jethava has looked after the tree there, with a copy on display at the Chorwad Mata Mandir for visiting families.
  4. Together
    Vimal Kishore Jethwa of Khambhalia gathered old family photographs and documents, including the original handwritten tree.
  5. Everywhere
    Families in India and around the world sent photographs, names, dates and memories, and continue to do so today.

Before you read

Words to know

Gotraગોત્ર
A family line traced back to a rishi (sage). Ours is Vasishtha.
Kuldevકુળદેવ
The family goddess who protects the line. Ours is Chorwad Mata. Also written Kuldevi.
Nivadનિવેદ
The family’s food offering to the Kuldev, followed by a shared meal. Also nived or naivedya.
Pedhiપેઢી
A generation. Dewji Bapa is pedhi 1 (P1); his sons are P2.
Barotબારોટ
The hereditary genealogists who kept the family’s records.
Surapuraસુરાપુરા
A brave ancestor honoured as a protector of the family. Ours is Kachra Bapa at Morzar.
Paliyaપાળિયો
A memorial stone raised for a hero.
Vavવાવ
A stepwell, like the Vikia Vav at Ghumli.

How sure are we?

Reading the labels

Throughout the archive, small labels show where a statement comes from.

Documented
Seen in a record, plaque, photograph or printed document.
Published history
From published histories; the source number is shown in [brackets].
Family record
From our family tree and the recollection of family members.
Tradition
Religious or community tradition, told and practised over generations.
Legend
A story handed down in the clan; not historical record.
Estimate
Worked out from the generations; not a recorded date.
Unresolved
Not yet known. Please help us confirm it.

Names to confirm

Some pairs of names may be the same person:

Ladha / Vaga · Kaba / Kala · Aata / Chatu · Babulal / Batuk · Krupesh / Kalpesh · Parijit / Parimal · Valji / Palji · Naran / Narad

The records also differ on the son of Jayesh (Amratlal), and there may be two different people called Dharmesh. Also unresolved: where Kachra Bapa, our Sura Pura Bapa, fits in the tree, and the quantities in the nivad list.

Now it’s your turn

Add your chapter

Nine centuries of Jethwas carried this name to you. Now you are the next flame.

  1. Find yourselfTrace your name back to Dewji Bapa in the tree and count your pedhi. Are you P11 or P12?
  2. Ask one storySit with your grandparents and ask them about a name in this archive. Record it on your phone.
  3. VisitLight a diya at our Kuldev in Khambhalia and pay your respects to Sura Pura Bapa at Morzar.
  4. Send it inA photo, a name, a correction or a memory. If you are not in the tree yet, send your name, your father’s name and his father’s name. Quote the # number when correcting someone.
