Welcome

A note from the compiler

This book did not begin with me, and it will not end with me. It is the work of many hands, and of many hearts.

It gathers in one place what our elders passed down: the names of our line from Dewji Bapa to today’s children, the shrines of our Kuldev and our Sura Pura Bapa, and the history of the Jethwa name in Saurashtra. For generations these lived only in memory. Writing them down is our way of keeping them safe.

Even so, a name may be misspelled or a relationship recorded wrongly. For any such error I ask your forgiveness, and your help to put it right.

Manoj Ramniklal Jethwa · Compiler · Jai Mataji · Jai Hanuman

Where do I fit?

Every person in this archive is connected to Dewji Bapa.

  1. Find your nameSearch the family tree. If you are not listed yet, look up your father, mother or grandparent. Married-in spouses can be found too.
  2. Read your pedhiEvery name carries a P number, its pedhi or generation. Dewji Bapa is P1 and his sons are P2. Most of today’s children are P11 or P12.
  3. Trace your lineChoose a name and the archive draws the line from Dewji Bapa down to that person, parent by parent.
  4. Add your chapterSend a photo, a name or a memory, so the next edition is more complete because of you. How to send it.
